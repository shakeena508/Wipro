📅 Day 2 – Cinema Hall Case Study (Part 2)
Topics Covered
Aggregate functions
GROUP BY
HAVING
Booking analysis
Brief Explanation

GROUP BY
Groups rows having the same value.

SELECT movie_name, COUNT(*)
FROM Bookings
GROUP BY movie_name;

HAVING
Filters grouped data.

SELECT movie_name, COUNT(*)
FROM Bookings
GROUP BY movie_name
HAVING COUNT(*) > 10;

📅 Day 15 – JOIN Revision
Topics Covered
INNER JOIN
LEFT JOIN
RIGHT JOIN
FULL JOIN
SELF JOIN
Simple Explanation

INNER JOIN → Matching records only.

SELECT *
FROM Students
INNER JOIN Courses
ON Students.course_id = Courses.course_id;

LEFT JOIN → All rows from the left table and matching rows from the right.

SELECT *
FROM Students
LEFT JOIN Courses
ON Students.course_id = Courses.course_id;
